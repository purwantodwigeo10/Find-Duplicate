about=Find Duplicate checks one selected key field in a point, line, or polygon layer and labels every feature as DUPLICATE IDENTIFIED or UNIQUE IDENTIFIED.
    The output keeps the key field and optional selected fields, adds frequency and location details, preserves the source geometry and CRS, and safely replaces an existing Shapefile only after the new output passes validation. No third-party Python packages or bundled binaries are required. Two successful trial runs are available before activation is required.
